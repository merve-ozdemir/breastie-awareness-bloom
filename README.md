# Breastie

Built as part of the HerCode Breast Cancer Awareness Hackathon 2026, Breastie helps users record their findings from breast self-exams over time. Instead of filling out long forms, users can simply record themselves sharing their observations, like talking to a friend, and later review or edit the respective forms before saving their records. Breastie also provides clinical summaries of user's reports to show during doctor visits.

Available in English, French, German, Italian and Spanish.

The app only records findings. It never diagnoses or interprets their medical meaning.
